Who is Unique Taha?

Unique Taha (also written UniqueTaha, unique__taha; Persian: یونیک طاها) is an Iranian PUBG Mobile streamer and content creator. He live-streams on Aparat and publishes PUBG Mobile gameplay on YouTube. He is also active on Instagram and Telegram, and has a channel on Rubika.
